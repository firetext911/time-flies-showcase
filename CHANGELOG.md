# Development Updates

Public progress on **Time Flies: Living Clock™**. Time Flies has not been released yet. These notes follow development toward the first App Store release.

## October 2026: Live Weather and Preview 🧪 *in testing*

- **ZIP-code weather.** Enter your ZIP code and the scene follows your real local weather, with no GPS or location permission needed.
- **First-launch setup.** A short welcome asks for your ZIP code, Fahrenheit or Celsius, and 12 or 24-hour time, then goes straight to the clock.
- **Sunrise and sunset.** Lighting follows the actual sunrise and sunset times for your area.
- **Weather Preview.** Try six presets at any time: Sunny, Rain, Snow, Thunderstorm, Moonlight, and Sunset. The real clock keeps running, and one tap returns you to live weather.
- **Offline-friendly.** When you're offline, the last weather stays visible and is clearly marked as out of date.

## October 2026: Mountain Cabin Experience ✅

- The signature view: looking out from a mountain cabin.
- **Framed** and **frameless** window styles.
- Rain falling outside, plus droplets that gather, slide, and leave trails down the glass.
- The clock sits clearly over the scenery in both orientations.

*Note: the current scenery is a hand-built stand-in. Final photographic scenery is being prepared.*

## October 2026: Always-On Clock ✅

- Full-screen clock that stays visible while the app is open.
- Customize the font, size, weight, color, and position.
- 12 or 24-hour time, and optional seconds, date, and temperature.
- Portrait and landscape.
- **Nightstand Mode**, with a large dimmed clock and minimal detail.
- Preferences are saved on your iPad.

## October 2026: Foundation ✅

- Project set up, with the app running on iPad.
