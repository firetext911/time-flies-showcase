# Community Guidelines

Time Flies is built on a simple idea: *Time Connects Us.* We want our Discussions to feel the same: warm, welcoming, and helpful.

## Please do

- Be kind and patient. People of all ages and backgrounds are here.
- Search before posting, and add to an existing idea when one fits.
- Keep each post to one idea or question, so it's easier to discuss and vote on.
- Explain *why* a feature would help you. It's often more useful than *how* to build it.

## Please don't

- Post harassment, hate, or personal attacks.
- Spam, advertise, or post off-topic promotion.
- Share anyone's personal information, including your own address or phone number.
- Post security vulnerabilities publicly. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
- Use Discussions for weather emergencies. Follow official alerts and local authorities.

## Moderation

Moderators may edit, lock, hide, or remove posts that break these guidelines, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the post.

## A note on ideas

We read every suggestion, but we can't promise to build every one. Upvotes help us understand what matters most to you. Posting an idea grants us permission to use it, as described in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
