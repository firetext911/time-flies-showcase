# Security Policy

This repository contains only public product information. The Time Flies: Living Clock™ app and its services are developed and maintained privately.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security issue in the Time Flies app, its website, or any related service, please report it **privately**:

1. Go to the **[Security tab](../../security)** of this repository.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability** to open a private advisory.

No GitHub account? Email **[timefliesbyfiretext@gmail.com](mailto:timefliesbyfiretext@gmail.com)** with "Security" in the subject line instead.

Please **do not** report security issues in Discussions, issues, or any other public place.

Helpful details include:

- what you found and where (app version, iPadOS version, or URL)
- steps to reproduce it
- the impact you think it could have

## What to expect

- We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days.
- We will keep you informed as we investigate and fix the issue.
- With your permission, we are happy to credit you once a fix is released.

## Scope

**In scope:** the Time Flies: Living Clock™ iPad app, this showcase repository, and its public website.

**Out of scope:** third-party services such as Apple, GitHub, and weather data providers. Please report those to the provider directly.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption, and that gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before anything is disclosed.

## Weather safety

Time Flies is not an emergency alert system and is not a replacement for Wireless Emergency Alerts, NOAA Weather Radio, or local emergency services.
