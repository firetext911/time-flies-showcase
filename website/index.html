Introducing

More than a clock. A window.

Time Flies: Living Clock™ turns your iPad into a calm, always-on clock surrounded by a living window scene. Rain trickles down the glass, snow drifts past the pines, and the light changes from sunrise to moonlight. Set it on a nightstand, kitchen counter, or desk and let it run.

On iPad

Designed for the big screen

Portrait or landscape, framed window or full-bleed scenery. The clock always comes first.

Features

Everything a clock should be, and a little more

Always-On Clock

A large, full-screen clock that stays visible while the app is open. No timeouts, no slideshows ending, no dashboards.

Make It Yours

Choose the font, size, weight, color, and position. 12 or 24-hour time, seconds, date, and temperature, all optional.

Living Window Scenery

A mountain cabin view with framed and frameless window styles, and lighting that follows the time of day.

Rain on the Glass

Our signature effect: droplets gather, slide, and leave trails down the window, while rain falls beyond it.

Live Local Weather

Enter a ZIP code and the scene follows your actual weather, from sunshine to snow. No location permission needed.

Weather Preview

Don't wait for a storm. Preview sunny, rain, snow, thunderstorm, moonlight, and sunset scenes any time, free.

Nightstand Mode

A large, dimmed clock with minimal detail and gentler animation, made for the bedroom.

Official Weather Alerts Planned

Official U.S. weather warnings for your area, free for everyone. A helpful extra, not a replacement for emergency alert systems.

Accessible by Design

VoiceOver, high contrast, and Reduce Motion support, with a calm static scene available.
