# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Time Flies: Living Clock™?
An always-on, full-screen clock for iPad. The clock sits in front of a living window scene that follows your real weather and the time of day.

### When will it be available?
It's in active development. Follow [Announcements](../../../discussions/categories/announcements) to hear about the launch first.

### Which devices are supported?
iPad, running iPadOS 17 or later.

### Will it have ads?
No. Time Flies is 100% ad-free on every plan, including Free. That will not change.

### Is it free?
Yes. The free version includes the always-on clock, essential customization, live weather, the Mountain Cabin scene, Weather Preview, and (when released) official weather alerts. An optional Pro upgrade will add extra features such as premium landscapes, ambient sounds, and holiday themes. Pricing will be announced closer to launch.

### Do I need an account?
No. Open the app, enter a ZIP code, and the clock runs. Connecting any account is optional.

### Does it need my location?
No. Time Flies uses the ZIP code you enter, not GPS.

### Will my iPad screen stay on?
While Time Flies is open and Always-On is enabled, the app keeps the screen from going to sleep on its own. It can't override you locking your iPad or iPadOS system restrictions. For long sessions, we recommend keeping your iPad plugged in.

### Does it work offline?
Yes. The clock always keeps running. Weather needs an internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular). When you're offline, the last weather is shown and marked as out of date.

### Can I see rain or snow when it isn't raining or snowing?
Yes. **Weather Preview** lets anyone try Sunny, Rain, Snow, Thunderstorm, Moonlight, and Sunset scenes at any time.

### Can I rely on it for severe weather warnings?
Official weather alerts are planned as a free, helpful extra. However, Time Flies is **not** an emergency alert system. Always keep Wireless Emergency Alerts enabled, and use NOAA Weather Radio and local authorities for emergencies.

### Is it available outside the United States?
The initial release focuses on the United States, since weather uses U.S. ZIP codes.

### Is the app open source?
No. Time Flies is proprietary software. This repository shares product information and hosts our community discussions, but it does not contain the app's source code.
