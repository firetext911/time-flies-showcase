# Features

**Time Flies: Living Clock™** is an always-on, full-screen clock for iPad, set in a living window of weather and light.

Status key: ✅ built · 🧪 in testing · 🗓️ planned. Planned features may change before release.

## The clock comes first

| Feature | Status |
|---|---|
| Large, full-screen clock that stays visible while the app is open | ✅ |
| No automatic timeouts, dashboards, or slideshows ending | ✅ |
| Customizable font, weight, size, color, and position | ✅ |
| 12 or 24-hour time | ✅ |
| Optional seconds, date, temperature, and weather description | ✅ |
| Portrait and landscape | ✅ |
| Keeps running without an internet connection | ✅ |
| Settings appear with a gentle tap and stay out of the way | ✅ |

> Time Flies keeps the screen awake while the app is open and Always-On is enabled. It cannot override you locking your iPad or iPadOS system restrictions.

## Live local weather 🧪

- Enter a U.S. ZIP code. No GPS or location permission is needed.
- Weather refreshes regularly while the app is open.
- Sunrise and sunset times for your area set the lighting.
- When you're offline, the last weather stays visible and is clearly marked as out of date.

## Weather Preview 🧪 *free for everyone*

See any scene without waiting for the weather: **Sunny, Rain, Snow, Thunderstorm, Moonlight, Sunset.**
The real clock keeps running, your location stays the same, and one tap returns you to live weather.

## Official weather alerts 🧪 *free for everyone*

- Official U.S. weather watches, warnings, and advisories for your area, from the National Weather Service.
- Checked about every 5 minutes while Time Flies is open.
- A clear banner beside the clock that never covers or replaces it.
- Full details: the issuing office, severity, urgency, affected area, timing, and the official instructions, word for word.
- VoiceOver announces new severe and extreme alerts.
- Choose which alerts appear (all, moderate and above, or severe and above), with a 7-day alert history. **Extreme alerts always appear.**
- A status indicator shows when alerts haven't been checked recently.

> Time Flies is not a replacement for Wireless Emergency Alerts, NOAA Weather Radio, or local emergency services.
